# Paper Days
Photographic fashion cutouts inhabit a tactile editorial scrapbook. Loose newspaper title pieces, understated small labels, original face and outfit as focal point. Architecture acts as scenery, not a real street perspective. A giant printed book page changes the world.

Colors: #eee9dd ivory paper; #d8dfeb powder blue; #e9d786 warm yellow; #222b37 ink; #315b82 blue accent.
Typography: Georgia is printed literary matter, Arial is functional labels; separate letters imitate physical offcuts.
Motion: decisive adhesive landings, gentle 1 degree settle, a single directional paper wipe with fully opaque coverage. Finish on a stable readable page.
Avoid: web cards, synthetic gradients, floating UI, face deformation, fake walking, identical movement on all layers.
